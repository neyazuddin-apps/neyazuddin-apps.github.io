Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Welcome to our Official App Store. This Privacy Policy applies to all applications provided by us, including but not limited to V Lite Player, Domino Score, Note Diary, Privacy Guard, and PUBG Turbo. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Our applications are designed to function primarily offline and do not require you to create an account. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data (such as name, email, or contacts) to our servers.

2. Local Device Permissions

To provide core functionalities, our apps may request specific device permissions:
• Storage/Media: Required by V Lite Player to access videos and Note Diary to save files.
• Overlay/Draw over other apps: Required by Privacy Guard and V Lite Player (for PiP mode).
• Device Administrator: Required by Privacy Guard to prevent unauthorized uninstallation.
All data processed via these permissions remains securely on your local device.

3. Log Data and Analytics

In the case of an app crash, we may collect Log Data (through third-party products) on your phone. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our service, the time and date of your use, and other non-personal statistics to help us fix bugs.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

Our applications and website use third-party services (such as GitHub for hosting and APK distribution). These external platforms have their own Privacy Policies. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

5. Security of Your Data

We value your trust in providing us your information, which is why apps like Note Diary and Privacy Guard use advanced local encryption. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy or data handling practices, please feel free to reach out to us via our official GitHub repository or community channels.
