Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Neyazuddin Apps

Welcome to Neyazuddin Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how data and device permissions are handled across our website and Android applications—V Lite Player, Domino Score, Note Diary, Privacy Guard, PUBG Turbo, Driver Diary, and Fee Diary.

1. Offline-First & Zero-Cloud Applications

  • Note Diary: Operates 100% offline with zero cloud storage. Hidden notes, photos, videos, PINs, and Security Questions remain solely on your device's internal storage using AES-256 encryption. Encrypted ZIP backups are generated locally only when you tap Create Backup.
  • Privacy Guard: Requires no internet permission and collects zero personal or screen data. Custom curtain photos are accessed locally using secure Android URI permissions.
  • V Lite Player & PUBG Turbo: Designed for offline media playback and offline device status/DND utilities without transmitting your personal files or passwords.

2. Device Permissions Explained

  • Storage / Media Access: Used by V Lite Player to list/play local videos and music, Note Diary to encrypt/hide vault files and export ZIP backups, Privacy Guard to load a user-selected curtain photo, and Fee Diary to save/share student ID cards and receipts.
  • Display Over Other Apps (Overlay): Used by Privacy Guard for the privacy curtain and V Lite Player for Picture-in-Picture / floating playback.
  • Biometric / Fingerprint: Used locally by Note Diary only if you enable Fingerprint Unlock in Settings.
  • Phone / DND Access (Optional): Used by PUBG Turbo only when you explicitly turn on Gaming DND or Ghost Mode to silence or reject calls during matches.

3. Optional Online & Cloud Sync Features

  • Driver Diary & Fee Diary: Support optional Google Firebase cloud backup where configured so you can store and restore your trip/diesel logs or student fee records across devices.
  • Domino Score: Works offline by default; uses internet only if you choose to Host or Join a Live Match Sync room using a custom Match ID.

4. Crash Logs & Third-Party Services

Apps using Firebase or connected features may log standard non-personal crash diagnostics (device model, Android OS version, timestamp) to help fix bugs. This website uses Google Fonts, Font Awesome, QR generation, and hosts APKs on GitHub Releases, which operate under their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Security & User Control

Because Note Diary does not upload your vault to any server, we cannot access or reset a forgotten Real PIN remotely. Always set a Security Question and store encrypted ZIP backups safely in Google Drive or your PC.

6. Changes & Contact

We may update this policy as apps evolve or launch on Google Play Store. For questions or support, email neyazuddin8882@gmail.com.
